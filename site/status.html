Maintenance

Restarts for updates happen on Sundays around 03:00 UTC and take under a minute. In-flight transcodes resume from their last completed segment; API calls during the window get a 503 with Retry-After.

Known issues

Interlaced 1080i sources with broken field order metadata come out with combing artefacts on the 480p rung. Workaround is an explicit deinterlace = true in the profile. Proper detection is on the list.

Very long sources (over about six hours) push the playlist past what some older TV browsers will parse. Splitting them at ingest is the current advice.
